## Context

What is the situation that forced this decision? What was unclear, what was at stake, what alternatives were on the table?

This section should give a reader who arrives in two years' time enough background to understand why the question even came up. Link to relevant design docs, prior ADRs, and any external sources that shaped the thinking.

If this ADR resolves an entry in `../design/open-questions.md`, name it explicitly here.

## Decision

What was decided? State it unambiguously, in one or two short paragraphs.

A reader skimming the ADRs to understand the current shape of the system should be able to read just this section and the title and know what is in force.

## Alternatives considered

The other options that were on the table, and why each was rejected. One short paragraph per alternative is usually enough. If an alternative was strong and might come back later, say so — that signposts where future ADRs may revisit the question.

## Consequences

What becomes easier as a result of this decision. What becomes harder. What new questions are now open. What existing code, docs, or ADRs need to be updated.

Be honest about the downsides. An ADR that lists only benefits is suspicious; every real decision has trade-offs, and recording them is part of the value.

## References

Links to relevant design docs, code, issues, external articles, prior ADRs.
